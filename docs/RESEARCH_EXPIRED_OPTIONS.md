## 6. Best realistic route (Q5)

1. **Upstox Plus available**: activate Plus (brokerage Rs 30/order while on Plus), run `probe`, then `continue` month by month back to ~June 2025 (data is "consistent from 15 June 2025"). This gives 1-minute OHLC + volume + OI for every expired weekly/monthly contract in the band.
2. **No Plus**: run `python -m updl record` every trading day (free Analytics token). It saves the full live chain incl. bid/ask/IV/greeks for the nearest expiry every minute. Start now: history before the first recorded day can only come from Plus.
3. **End-of-day cross-check**: NSE F&O UDiFF bhavcopy (`python -m updl ingest <zip or folder>`), owner-downloaded; updl never scrapes NSE.
